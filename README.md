### Hi, I'm Dylan 👋

Business student (Michigan Ross) recruiting for product management and quant roles. I like building small, opinionated tools that fix a specific annoyance in my own workflow — the two below are the current favorites.

**[Job Application Tracker](https://github.com/dylnwng/job-application-tracker)** — a Chrome extension that captures job postings straight from the active tab into a local Kanban dashboard, built after getting tired of paywalled trackers and manual copy-paste. No backend, no account — everything lives in the browser.

**[Second Brain](https://github.com/dylnwng/second-brain-template)** — a pattern for using an LLM agent as the maintainer of a persistent personal knowledge base, not just a chatbot answering questions against uploaded files. The interesting part isn't "I used AI" — it's the guardrails: explicit data-sufficiency thresholds before the system will claim a trend, and a hard separation between what I actually said and what the AI researched on its own.
